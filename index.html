Editorial

Why a doctor, not a kit?

A home kit gives you numbers. It doesn't tell you what they mean, whether they matter, or what to do about them. It sits in a drawer — or a browser tab — unread and unactioned.

helfy® is the opposite. A named, GMC-registered doctor chooses your tests, reviews your results, explains them in plain English, and writes a plan built around your bloods — not a population average.

Readers Ask

The questions you haven't said out loud

My GP already does an annual check.+

A standard NHS check covers a baseline — cholesterol, blood sugar, blood pressure. It's not designed to catch visceral fat, age-appropriate testosterone, or an early pre-diabetic trend. helfy® fills exactly that gap, without replacing your GP.

What if you find something I can't unfind?+

Finding it early is the entire point. You don't get handed a number and left to Google it — you get a doctor and a written plan to address it. The trajectory is almost always reversible when it's caught now, not in five years.

Is this covered by my health cash plan?+

Most health cash plans reimburse helfy®. Almost nobody checks. We'll help you confirm your cover before you book — often the difference between "expensive" and "already paid for".
